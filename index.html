The Sunday review (20 to 30 minutes)

Keep

Themes with strong relevant reach, saves, shares, meaningful comments or qualified enquiries.

Improve

Good reach but weak engagement, unclear hooks, low readability, vague calls to action, slow follow-up.

Stop or test

Formats that cost too much time for little signal, unsupported claims, broad posts that speak to nobody.

Review the data, name one learning, choose next week's priorities, and assign owners and due dates.

Guardrails and launch readiness

The quality bar: accurate, useful, visually clear, permissioned, audience-specific and action-oriented.

Brand, quality and risk guardrails

  • Use only approved Tektork logo files. Never redraw, distort or recolour the logo without approval.
  • Keep a consistent blue-and-white look, readable text and mobile-safe margins.
  • Verify technical details, programme claims, dates, affiliations and results before publishing.
  • Get consent for identifiable students, testimonials, institution names and photos.
  • Never publish confidential student data, internal dashboards, credentials or sensitive partner information.
  • Avoid fake metrics, guaranteed placement claims, exaggerated outcomes and misleading demonstrations.
  • Respond professionally to criticism. Escalate complaints, safety issues and factual corrections to the campaign owner.
